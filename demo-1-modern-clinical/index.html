Patient Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about treatments, Myofascial Release, and house visits at Aadhira Wellness.

Myofascial Release (MFR) is a specialized hands-on therapy that releases restrictions within the tough connective tissues (fascia) wrapping your muscles. If you suffer from chronic neck tightness, fibromyalgia, persistent lower back stiffness, or unresolved sports injuries, Dr. Santhosh Balaji's MFR therapy eliminates deep trigger points and restores pain-free mobility.
For elderly individuals, post-stroke recovery, post-fracture patients, or anyone unable to travel to our Kundrathur center, we provide comprehensive home physiotherapy visits across Chennai. We bring portable electrotherapy, IFT, TENS, and exercise equipment right to your bedside.
Dr. Santhosh Balaji performs a targeted physical assessment of your joints, posture, radiating pain vectors, and muscle tightness. You receive your immediate pain relief protocol and a customized milestone recovery plan.
We are open Monday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 9:00 PM. Sunday appointments and house visits can be arranged with prior booking.
If you have recent X-rays, MRI reports, or doctor prescription sheets, please bring them along. However, they are not mandatory; we conduct thorough physical and orthopedic clinical tests during your evaluation.
