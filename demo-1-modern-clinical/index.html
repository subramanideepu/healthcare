Patient Information

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know prior to your first clinical physiotherapy assessment at Adhira Wellness.

During your initial 45-minute consultation, our licensed physiotherapist will take a comprehensive clinical history, conduct specific orthopedic and neurological mobility tests, and identify the root cause of your pain. You will also receive your initial hands-on treatment and a clear recovery milestone roadmap.
No referral is strictly required for private self-paying patients; you can schedule directly with us. However, if you have prior MRI scans, X-rays, or operative summaries from your orthopedic surgeon or physician, please bring them along so we can integrate them into your clinical file.
Every condition is unique. Acute muscular strains typically show significant resolution within 3 to 6 sessions. Complex chronic disc issues or post-operative joint rehabilitation often require an 8 to 12-week structured progressive protocol. We document improvements weekly so you can see your functional gains.
Please wear loose, comfortable athletic clothing (such as track pants, shorts, or a t-shirt) that permits easy examination and movement of your affected joints and spine.
Clinical studies show that over 85% of lumbar and cervical disc herniations respond favorably to conservative, evidence-based physiotherapy, including computerized traction, mechanical decompression, and core stabilization, eliminating the necessity for invasive spinal surgery.

Adhira Wellness is an evidence-led physiotherapy and rehabilitation clinic committed to restoring human movement, functional independence, and holistic health through clinical precision.
